Mesopotomia

c. 3500 BCE – 539 BCE

The land between two rivers. Stretching across the fertile crescent of the Tigris and Euphrates, Mesopotamia gave birth to the world's first cities. It was here that humanity invented the wheel, forged the first codes of written law under Hammurabi, and pressed the first cuneiform letters into wet clay.
