The Tapestry of Time

A chronological map of human ambition.

c. 3500 BCE

The First Cities: Uruk arises in Mesopotamia. Humanity invents the wheel and the first form of writing (cuneiform).

c. 3100 BCE

Unification of Egypt: Narmer unites Upper and Lower Egypt, beginning the first dynastic rule along the Nile.

c. 2560 BCE

The Great Pyramid: Construction finishes on the tomb of Khufu, standing as the tallest human-made structure for 3,800 years.

c. 1180 BCE

Bronze Age Collapse: A mysterious, catastrophic systems failure wipes out empires across the Mediterranean. Troy falls.

27 BCE

The Roman Empire: Augustus Caesar becomes the first emperor, ending the Republic and beginning the Pax Romana.
