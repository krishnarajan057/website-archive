Ancient Greek

c. 1600 BCE – 146 BCE

A fragmented landscape of fiercely independent city-states, Greece laid the intellectual foundation for Western civilization. From the harsh military discipline of Sparta to the democratic experiment of Athens, the Greeks pioneered philosophy, drama, geometry, and a mythology so potent it still shapes modern storytelling.
