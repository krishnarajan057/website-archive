Rome

c. 753 BCE – 476 CE

What began as a small settlement on the Palatine Hill grew into a superpower that dominated the Mediterranean basin for centuries. Rome was an empire of engineers, lawyers, and soldiers. They built roads that connected continents, aqueducts that tamed nature, and a republic that eventually fell to the absolute power of emperors.
